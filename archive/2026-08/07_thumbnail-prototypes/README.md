# Thumbnail Prototypes Archive

Archived on 2026-08-07.

## Contents

This bundle preserves the Hub-card thumbnail candidate generations from the A-S series (the A-F, J-N, and P-S variants present in the prototype set), together with their PIL generator scripts. It also contains the former site design prototypes page.

The final thumbnail is the Q5 variant, committed as `site/.face/thumbnail.png`. Its generation path is preserved in `generate-Q.py` and `generate-final.py`.

## Previous Locations

- Thumbnail candidates and generators: `thumbnail-prototypes/` at the repository root
- Site design prototypes page: `site/prototypes.html`

## Regeneration

`site/.face/thumbnail.png` can be regenerated with `generate-final.py` from this archive bundle.
